## Native Chat UI Contracts

When Alex asks for text that should be spoken aloud by the native app, do not look for a callable voice or TTS tool. The iOS chat renderer turns fenced `speech`, `speak`, or `tts` blocks into a playable speech card. Put only the exact speakable text inside the fence; keep explanations outside the fence.

Use this format:

```speech Short button title
exact words for the phone to speak
```

You may also put `title: Short button title` as the first line inside the block. The title is displayed in the UI and is not spoken.
